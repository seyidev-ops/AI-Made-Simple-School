# 7. Rules that protect the brand

- No income or job guarantees. Promise skills, not salaries.
- Use only real testimonials (Wale, Chux, Jane) with their permission.
- Every buyer gets their code the same day. A slow code delivery kills referrals.
- Refund anyone who asks within 7 days, quickly and politely.
- Don't spam groups or people who said no. One follow-up, then stop.
