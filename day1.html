Part 1 · 10 minutes · Learn

Persona, Context, Task

Most people type "write posts for my shop" and get generic results. AI isn't bad at writing; it just doesn't know anything about you. Give it three things and the answers change completely.

PersonaWho the AI should be. "You are a friendly sales assistant for a home bakery."
ContextWho you serve and what matters. "My customers are busy mums ordering birthday cakes in Abuja."
TaskExactly what you want back. "Write 7 posts, under 40 words each, with one call to action."
Weak promptWrite some posts for my cake business.
Strong promptYou are a friendly sales assistant for Ada's Cakes, a home bakery. My customers are busy mums and office workers in Abuja who order birthday cakes and small chops. Write 7 days of Instagram captions, Monday to Sunday, each under 40 words, warm Nigerian English, ending with "Order on WhatsApp".

The strong version takes 30 seconds longer to type, and saves you rewriting everything the AI gives back.

Part 3 · 5 minutes · Apply

Make it yours

  1. Pick the best three of the seven posts.
  2. Change one line in each so it sounds like you: a customer's name, a price, a local landmark.
  3. Post Monday's one now, or schedule it. Save the rest in your notes.
  4. Not happy with a post? Reply to the AI: "Make number 4 shorter and add a price." Asking for changes is part of the skill.
